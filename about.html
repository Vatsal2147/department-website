OUR IDENTITY

From a centre to a department shaping environmental research.

The Department of Environmental Science and Engineering at IIT Bombay stands as a pioneer in interdisciplinary environmental education and cutting-edge solutions.

Established as a dedicated Centre in 1985, the entity officially achieved Department status in February 2019. Driven by a core faculty of 18 multidisciplinary experts, ESED bridges science and engineering to solve pressing local and global ecological challenges.

Core Research Domains

01

Clean Technologies & Industrial Pollution Prevention

Sustainable industrial processes reducing emissions at the source.

02

Hazardous Waste Treatment & Disposal

Safe confinement, transformation, and neutralization of toxic solids.

03

Biomedical Waste Management

Sterilization, handling protocols, and non-polluting waste destruction.

04

Industrial Wastewater Biodegradation

Biological treatment for complex organic industrial effluents.

05

Non-Aqueous Liquid Pollutants

Mitigating hydrophobic solvents, oils, and persistent organic liquids.

06

Desalination & Membrane Technology

Advanced membrane filtration for clean water recovery and brackish treatment.

07

Toxicity & Mutagenicity Testing

Bio-assay evaluation of industrial chemicals and environmental samples.

08

Novel Bioreactors

High-efficiency biological reactor design for bioprocess engineering.

09

Quantifying atmospheric particulates and human exposure risk assessment.

10

Tracing air contaminants to chemical origins using isotopic and molecular signatures.

Comprehensive ecological risk analysis for infrastructure and industry.

12

Restoration techniques for contaminated soils, groundwater, and natural ecosystems.
