1977

The Beginning

ESED began in 1977 as an interdisciplinary group bringing together faculty from across IIT Bombay. In 1985, it became the Centre for Environmental Science and Engineering. After decades of research and teaching, the Centre gained department status in 2019. Today, ESED continues that interdisciplinary legacy through science and engineering.

1964

Specialisations Evolve

Environmental challenges have changed — and so has ESED. What began with a strong focus on pollution control and environmental quality has expanded into climate change, resource recovery, environmental biotechnology, modelling and data science. The department now tackles problems across multiple scales, from local pollution to global environmental change.

1996

Dual Degree Introduced

ESED has continually evolved the way students learn and conduct environmental research. Its M.Sc.–Ph.D. dual degree combines advanced coursework with long-term research. A B.Tech.–M.Tech. dual degree was introduced in 2018 before evolving into a standalone B.Tech. programme from 2023. Together, these programmes reflect ESED's emphasis on both engineering and research.

PRESENT

Course Work

ESED's work spans a wide range of interconnected environmental challenges. Its research can be broadly viewed through Air & Atmosphere, Water & Wastewater, Waste & Circularity, Environmental Biotechnology, and Climate & Data. These areas bring together science, engineering and technology to understand and solve complex environmental problems.

RESEARCH

World-Class Facilities

ESED supports its research with specialised laboratories for air quality, environmental chemistry, microbiology, waste management and instrumentation. Advanced facilities enable researchers to analyse pollutants, study biological processes and develop treatment technologies. From sophisticated analytical instruments to pilot-scale experiments, the department connects research with real-world environmental solutions.
